How to play

Explore the forest and crypt, pull goblins and skeletons, and hunt named monsters for unique items. Summon an earth pet to tank, use fire bolts and damage-over-time magic, then replace your wounded pet during battle. You can only cast while standing still. Rest to recover mana. Monsters remember who attacked them and nearby allies may assist.

Controls: WASD / arrow keys move; click ground to travel; click enemy to target; 1–8 spells; F pet attack; G follow; R meditate; Space nearest enemy; Esc cancel movement. Progress saves automatically in this browser.
